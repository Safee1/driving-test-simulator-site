What the app is — and is not

Driving Test Simulator is a practice and training aid. Mock tests are marked to the DVSA DL25 standard and ADI Part 3 assessments follow the DVSA scoring structure, but Driving Test Simulator is not a DVSA product or service, is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVSA, and nothing in the app is an official driving test or an official result. A pass or fail shown in the app is a practice estimate, not a prediction or guarantee of any outcome on a real DVSA test.

Routes and navigation — read this before you drive

In-app test routes and turn-by-turn guidance are generated from third-party mapping data (OpenStreetMap, OSRM, Google Maps) and checked at the desk — cross-referenced against map data and driven in simulation — not by a person physically driving them, unless a specific route is explicitly marked in the app as road-checked. Roads, signs, road layouts and restrictions can and do change after a route is built. You are solely responsible for observing the actual road, all traffic signs, signals and laws, and the instructions of any supervising driver or instructor with you, at all times, in preference to anything the app says. Never follow an in-app instruction that conflicts with the road in front of you, and never use the app in a way that takes your attention off driving safely. "Practice from here" routes are generated automatically and carry the same caveat.

Who can use it

The app is intended for learner drivers (normally 17+, in some cases 16 under the relevant DVSA rules) and driving instructors. If you drive using the app you must hold a valid provisional or full GB driving licence as required by law, and any practice or mock-test drive must be undertaken exactly as a real driving lesson would be — with a qualifying supervising driver or ADI, in a vehicle that meets legal requirements, insured for the purpose. The app does not supervise you and is not a substitute for a qualified supervising driver.
