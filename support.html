Driving Test Simulator (PassTrack) is a UK DVSA-style mock driving test app for Android: DL25 examiner marking, ADI Part 3 scoring, and in-app practice routes with turn-by-turn navigation.

FAQ

How do I start a mock test?

Open the app, choose a route near your test centre, and start the test when you're ready to drive. The app marks faults as you go and produces a DL25-style report at the end.

How do routes work?

Routes are provisional practice loops around real test centres. They are not official DVSA routes, and no route in the app is road-verified — use them for practice only, never as a guarantee of what your real test will involve.

How do I share a result?

Add a friend using a share code, then turn on result sharing (off by default) to let accepted friends see a summary of your mock test — route, date, pass/fail, score and fault counts — on a friends-only leaderboard. You can turn sharing off again at any time. Your full report, notes, signatures and location are never shared.

How do I delete my account?

See our account deletion page for instructions — in the app, or by email if you can't get in.
