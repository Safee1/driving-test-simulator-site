Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Who we are

Driving Test Simulator is operated by RDS HUB LTD (company no. 16285288), a UK driving school registered at 6 Exeter Place, Walsall, England, WS2 9UQ. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: info@rds-hub-ltd.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Name and email — to create and secure your account and show who a report belongs to. You can sign up with an email and password, or with your Google or Apple account — if you use Google or Apple, we only ever receive the name and email address they share with us, never your Google or Apple password.
  • Driving licence number (learner accounts) — your unique identity, and how an instructor who already knows it can send you a link request. Licence numbers can only be looked up exactly, never listed or searched, and only your name is returned to a requesting instructor. We keep a one-way scrambled (hashed) copy of each licence number used to sign up, so that the same licence number can't be used to create a second account — this check stays in place even if an account is later deleted, so a licence number is permanently tied to the first account it was used for.
  • Instructor email, for linking — a learner can send a link request to an instructor by entering the instructor's email address. Like licence numbers, an email can only be looked up exactly (never listed or searched), and only the instructor's name is returned so you can confirm the match before sending the request.
  • PRN and ADI badge photo (instructor accounts) — a one-time human check that you're a registered instructor. As with licence numbers, we keep a one-way scrambled (hashed) copy of each PRN used to sign up so it can only ever be tied to one account, permanently.
  • Friends and shared results (optional, off by default) — you can add friends with a share code. If you turn on result sharing, friends you have accepted can see a summary of each mock test — route, date, pass/fail, score and fault counts — on a friends-only leaderboard. Your full report, notes, signatures and location are never shared. Sharing stays off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off again at any time.
  • Address (optional) — off by default. When sharing is off, your address stays on your device only, and is removed from our servers if previously shared.
  • Mock test and assessment reports — the core service: fault markings, scores, notes, in-app signatures and PDFs, kept in your account history.
  • Crash reporting — if the app crashes or misbehaves, a technical report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics (with the related Firebase Sessions and Firebase Installations services) so we can find and fix the fault. A report contains the technical details of the failure (a stack trace), your device model, operating system version, the app version, and a random installation identifier for that copy of the app. It contains no account data, no report contents and no location. This is used only to keep the app working.
  • Location (optional, with your permission) — three uses. (1) When you allow it, the spot where each fault is marked during a test is saved with that fault, so the report can show it on a map. (2) During a mock test, the route you drive is recorded (about one point every few seconds, from the moment the test timer starts until it stops) and saved with that test's report so you can see your whole drive on a map afterwards. Fault locations and the driven route are part of your report and are deleted with it. (3) During route guidance and practice, your live position is used on your device to follow the map and speak instructions. For some features — one-tap "Practice from here" routes, being guided onto a route's start, and being routed back if you go off-route — your live position (and, briefly, a nearby target point) is sent to a routing service, OSRM (router.project-osrm.org, an open-source routing project, not Google; if that server does not answer, the same request goes to its mirror routing.openstreetmap.de, run by FOSSGIS e.V.), so it can calculate real road directions. We do not store that request, and it is never linked to your account or reports, but it does leave your device to reach OSRM's server, which may log it under its own terms; we use it only to calculate that one route. Apart from that, the live feed is not stored and stays on your device (a practice drive's route summary is shown once when you finish and is not saved). If you lock your screen or switch apps while guidance is running, guidance keeps talking so instructions don't cut out mid-drive — Android shows a persistent notification for as long as this is happening. (The app is currently available on Android only.) This uses the same one-time permission you already granted (we do not request "always allow" background location), only runs while guidance is switched on, and doesn't collect, store or send anywhere any data beyond what's described on this page. Say no to the permission and everything still works; reports simply have no map pins or route replay.

In the app, we do not collect advertising identifiers, and we use no advertising or behavioural analytics — the crash reporting described above is the only diagnostic data that leaves the app. We never track your location outside an active test or route guidance. The app is free and shows no ads.

Where your data lives

Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), stored in the London (europe-west2) region and encrypted in transit. Where any processing occurs outside the UK, it is protected by UK-approved safeguards (Google's standard contractual clauses). The app also keeps a copy on your device so it works offline.

Maps. Maps, route guidance and route replays are shown using Google Maps. When a map is displayed, your device requests map data from Google (and, during guidance, uses your location on-device to position the map). Google processes this under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform terms. We don't send Google your account details or your reports.

Route calculation. Some routing features — one-tap practice routes, turn-by-turn guidance onto a route's start, and automatic re-routing if you go off-route — send your live GPS coordinates to OSRM (router.project-osrm.org), a routing service run by the open-source OSRM project, so it can work out real road directions. If that server does not answer, the same request is sent to the OSRM mirror at routing.openstreetmap.de, run by FOSSGIS e.V. (an OpenStreetMap non-profit). We use OSRM only to calculate the route in that moment; it is not sent your account details or reports, and it is never linked to you. We do not control what either server itself logs or retains — its use is governed by the OSRM project's own terms.

Optional "Help improve routes" drive traces. After a drive, the app may ask once whether you want to help improve routes. Only if you say yes, and only while you are signed in, each finished drive's positions, speeds and the spoken instructions you were given are uploaded to our database (Google Firebase), linked to your account's user ID (not your name or email). Before upload, the first 150 metres and the last 150 metres of the drive are removed so the places you start and finish, such as home or work, are not stored; the remaining positions are rounded to about one metre. We use this only to improve route quality, the timing of spoken directions and speed-limit data. It is never shared with anyone else. You can switch it off at any time in Settings, and your drive traces are deleted when you delete your account. If you say no, nothing is uploaded.

"Take me back" and your saved home area. At the end of a drive you can ask for directions to the nearest test centre, a town or postcode you type, or a home area you have saved in Coaching settings. To turn a typed place or postcode into a map position, the app sends that text (not your identity) to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service; the route itself is then worked out by the routing service described above. A saved home area is kept only on your device — it is never uploaded to us — and it is removed if you clear it in Coaching settings, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

Map tiles for the in-car / phone map. To draw the street map itself (the background tiles, separate from route calculation above), the app downloads map tiles from driving-test-simulator.co.uk, which is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). Like any web request, the host receives the device's IP address and which map area is being requested — this is ordinary web-server access logging, and GitHub's own privacy statement applies to it. We don't receive or store this information ourselves. The Route preview feature (previewing a practice loop before you drive it) uses this same map and routing data already described above and sends nothing new or additional.

This website: cookies and analytics

This website (driving-test-simulator.co.uk) is separate from the app. The app uses no advertising or behavioural analytics — see above. On the website we ask, before anything runs, whether you'll allow two Google services: Google Analytics 4 (how many people visit, which pages, roughly which area they're in) and Google Ads conversion measurement (whether a visit that came from one of our adverts led to a tap on the Google Play button).

If you choose Reject, or don't choose, no Google script loads and no cookies are set — the site works exactly the same. If you Accept, Google sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*, _gcl_au and similar) lasting up to 13 months, and processes the data under the Google Privacy Policy; some processing may take place outside the UK under Google's standard contractual clauses. We don't use this data to identify you and we don't combine it with app accounts.

Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage, not a cookie) so we don't ask every visit, and you can change it any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer. Legal basis: consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PECR reg. 6).

Legal bases (UK GDPR)

Providing the service you asked for (contract); verifying instructor badges (legitimate interest — you can object to this at any time, see Your rights); optional address sharing and friends result-sharing (consent, off by default and withdrawable any time in the app).

How long we keep it

Your profile and reports are kept until you delete your account — nothing has a longer life than the account itself. Badge photos are kept while the account exists (they're re-checked if instructor status is questioned) and go with it on deletion. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days, and residual copies in system backups are purged within 30 days of deletion. You can delete in the app or via our account deletion page. PDFs you exported yourself remain under your control.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data in a portable format, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing, and you can complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk). Email us and we'll respond within one month.

Children

The app is intended for learner drivers (normally 17+, in some cases 16) and instructors. It is not directed at children under 16.

Sharing between users

Some features let you share with people you choose — never automatically:

  • Your instructor — an instructor you link with and accept can see your mock test reports and lesson records. You can remove the link at any time.
  • Lessons — an instructor can choose to share a lesson record with a linked student, and controls this for each lesson; an instructor's private notes are never shared with the student.
  • Friends — off by default. If you turn on result sharing, friends you accept see only a summary of your mock results (never the full report, notes, signatures or location). You can turn it off again any time.

We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes. Google (Firebase) processes data on our behalf under their data-processing terms. For the routing features described under "Route calculation" above, your live position is sent to OSRM, a third-party routing service, solely to calculate that route — never for OSRM's own purposes, never linked to your account, and never anything beyond the coordinates that one request needs.
