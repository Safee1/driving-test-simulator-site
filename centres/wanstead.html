Wanstead (London) driving test centre — practice routes

Getting ready for your practical test at Wanstead (London)? Driving Test Simulator has 3 provisional practice loops built around this test centre, so you can rehearse the kind of roads, junctions and roundabouts you're likely to meet on the day.

Practice loops at Wanstead (London)

3 provisional practice loops around Wanstead (London) test centre, built from map data — not the examiner's own routes, and not yet driven and checked on the road:

  • Route 1 — Snaresbrook, Lea Bridge Road & Woodford loop
  • Route 2 — Beehive Lane, Cranbrook Road & Aldersbrook loop
  • Route 5 — James Lane, Lea Bridge Road & Wood Street loop

How to practise

  1. Open the Driving Test Simulator app and pick Wanstead (London) from the list of test centres.
  2. Choose a practice loop, or start a DL25-style mock test.
  3. Drive only with a supervising driver in the car who can override the app's directions at any point — these are provisional loops, not routes that have been driven and checked.

Nearest other centres

  • Barking (Tanner Street) — 3.5 miles
  • Chingford (London) — 3.7 miles
  • Goodmayes — 3.9 miles
